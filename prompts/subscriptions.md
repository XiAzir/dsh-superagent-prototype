Tickets subscribe to their triggering threads and bound PR thread.
Subscribed messages from all participants and bots reach workers as context, regardless of mentions or sender checks; authority follows policy.
Workers handle human follow-ups and return unrelated work; Router gets follow_up and may answer status from saved evidence. QQ/Web status preserves task state; executable requests attach. Other events use policy admission.
Ended tickets retain subscriptions, even after PR closure or merge. Human follow-ups resume the same mandate; bots and CI stay asleep. A verified closure or merge ends bound tickets unless another ticket PR is open or resumed work remains unready.
Human-stopped tickets receive no further events; new work requires an accept through orchestrator_route.
orchestrator_thread_unsubscribe removes all ticket subscriptions on a thread; later positive routing can restore them.
Trigger dispositions retain filtering, assessment, routing and delivery history. After resolving intake, orchestrator_trigger_requeue reassesses a retained trigger.
Bots, CI, initial history and quotations supply context. Human requests and standing policy authorize work; ordinary discussion needs no reply or code change.
